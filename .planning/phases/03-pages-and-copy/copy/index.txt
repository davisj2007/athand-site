# H3
Your info, ready when you need it
# H4
atHand keeps the details you look up all the time, such as people, vehicles, pets, homes, financial accounts, and contacts, in one place on your iPhone.
# C1
Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.
# H5
For the moments you need it
# H6
At the doctor’s office, at a traffic stop, or at the airport, you need a member ID, a license number, or a passport number right away. atHand keeps them on your iPhone, for you and the people, vehicles, pets, and homes you look after.
# H7
What it does
# H8
Keep records for people, vehicles, pets, homes, financial accounts, and contacts, or make a custom record for anything else.
# H9
Scan a driver’s license, passport, insurance card, VIN, vaccine record, or business card instead of typing. You check every field before it is saved.
# H10
Get a suggestion for the record that is likely to help right now, based on the time of day and, if you allow them, your calendar and location.
# H11
Lock atHand with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode, and export an encrypted backup protected by a passphrase you choose.

# H13
Privacy in short
# H14
Your information stays on your iPhone. It leaves only inside an encrypted backup file that you choose to export.
# H15
There is no account and no sign-in.
# H16
atHand connects to Apple and RevenueCat for purchases, to Google for anonymous scanning diagnostics, and, if you allow location, to Apple to look up the name of a nearby place. It never sends your scans, photos, recognized text, or anything you enter.

The developer added no advertising, tracking, analytics, or crash-reporting tools.
