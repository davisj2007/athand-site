# P3
Pricing
# P4
atHand is a free download. A one-time purchase unlocks full use. There is no subscription, no ads, and no account.
# C1
Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.
# P5
Free
# P6
Without a purchase, you can:
# P7
Create, view, and edit your own record.
Scan documents and attach photos to your own record.
Use the app lock, appearance, and suggestion settings.
Export and import backups.
# P8
One-time purchase
# P9
The unlock adds:
# P10
Records for your household, vehicles, pets, homes, financial accounts, contacts, and custom records.
Scanning documents for every record, not just yours.
Opening every record, including records that arrive in a backup you import.
# P11
You pay once, and the unlock is yours to keep. The App Store shows the price in your local currency before you buy.
# P12
Your purchase and your Apple ID
# P13
A purchase belongs to the Apple ID you used to buy it. It unlocks every iPhone signed in to that Apple ID after you tap Restore purchase.
# P14
Someone with a different Apple ID, such as a partner, buys separately. Family Sharing is not available for the unlock.
# P15
Purchases are handled by Apple.
# P16
Backups never unlock
# P17
A backup file carries your information only, never the purchase. On a new iPhone, tap Restore purchase to unlock atHand with your Apple ID.
# P18
If you import a backup without the unlock, every record in it still appears on your home screen. Your own record stays free, and opening any other record asks you to unlock.
# P19
Questions about purchases? See Support.
