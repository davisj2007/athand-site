!Common questions
# S9
Backups
# S10
How do backups work?
# S11
You can export an encrypted backup file, protected by a passphrase you choose. You then share the file wherever you want to keep it. A backup is a one-time copy, not sync. The developer never receives the file.
# S12
How do I move atHand to a new iPhone?
# S13
On your old iPhone, open Settings in atHand, choose Backup & restore, and export a backup. Save it somewhere your new iPhone can reach, such as the Files app. On the new iPhone, import the backup and enter your passphrase. Then tap Restore purchase to unlock.
# S14
What happens when I import a backup?
# S15
Importing replaces all the data on that iPhone with the data in the backup. atHand warns you first and asks you to confirm with Replace all data on this device. If the passphrase is wrong, the import stops before anything changes.
# S16
How is my backup protected?
# S17
atHand encrypts the file with AES-256, using keys made from your passphrase with PBKDF2. An HMAC-SHA256 check means a changed or damaged file is rejected before it is opened.
# S18
What happens if I lose my backup passphrase?
The backup cannot be opened. The developer has no access to your passphrase and cannot recover it or the backup. Store your passphrase somewhere safe.
# S19
What does a backup include?
Your records and their photos. A backup never includes your purchase, your settings, or your app lock setting.
# S20
Purchases
# S21
How much does atHand cost?
# S22
atHand is a free download. The unlock is a one-time purchase, and the App Store shows its price in your local currency before you buy. See Pricing for what is free and what the unlock adds.
href: /pricing.html
# S23
How do I restore my purchase?
# S24
Open atHand on an iPhone signed in to the Apple ID you used to buy the unlock, then tap Restore purchase in Settings or on the unlock screen. If it does not work, email me.
# S25
What is free, and what does the unlock add?
# S26
Your own record is free, including scanning, the app lock, and backups. A one-time purchase unlocks records for your household, vehicles, pets, homes, financial accounts, contacts, and custom records. There is no subscription.
# S27
Can my family use my purchase?
# S28
A purchase covers every iPhone signed in to your Apple ID. Someone with a different Apple ID buys separately. Family Sharing is not available for the unlock.
# S29
Does importing a backup unlock atHand?
# S30
No. A backup carries your information only, never the purchase. Use Restore purchase on the new iPhone.
# S31
Why are some records locked after I import a backup?
# S32
Records other than your own need the unlock. They still appear on your home screen, and tapping one opens the unlock screen. If you already bought the unlock with this Apple ID, tap Restore purchase.
# S33
App lock
# S34
How do I turn on the app lock?
# S35
The app lock is on by default when your iPhone has Face ID, Touch ID, or a passcode set up. You can turn it off, or change how soon it locks again, in atHand’s Settings.
# S36
Is there an atHand password?
# S37
No. atHand uses Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode, never a separate password. Turning the lock on or off asks you to confirm with one of them.
# S38
Why can’t I turn on the app lock?
# S39
The lock needs Face ID, Touch ID, or a device passcode. If none is set up, set a passcode in iPhone Settings, then turn the lock on in atHand.
# S40
Scanning
# S41
Which documents can I scan?
Driver’s licenses, passports, insurance cards, VINs, vaccine records, utility bills, payment cards, and business cards.
# S42
What if a scan reads something wrong?
# S43
Every scan opens a check screen before anything is saved. atHand marks any field it is unsure of with “Check this.” Correct any field, or tap the photo to retake it.
# S44
Can I add a record without scanning?
Yes. When you add a record, choose Enter manually.
# S45
Are my scans sent anywhere?
# S46
No. Recognition runs on your iPhone, and atHand never sends your scans, photos, or the text read from them. The scanning library does send Google anonymous diagnostics, described in the privacy policy.
href: /privacy.html
# S47
Suggestions
# S48
How do suggestions work?
# S49
atHand compares signals such as the time of day and, if you allow them, your calendar and location with your records, on your iPhone. If you allow location, Apple’s geocoding service returns the name of a nearby place. When nothing matches with confidence, atHand shows no suggestion banner.
# S50
How do I turn suggestions off?
# S51
In atHand’s Settings, under Contextual Surfacing, set Context mode to Off.
# S52
Permissions and your data
# S53
Which permissions are optional?
Calendar and location access are both optional. atHand works without them, and you can change them at any time in iPhone Settings. Camera access for scanning and the app lock are also your choice. See the privacy policy for what each one does.
# S54
How do I delete all my data?
Delete the app from your iPhone. This removes all of its data from the device. Backup files you exported are stored separately, so delete those as well if you no longer want them.
# S55
Where can I read how atHand handles my information?
The privacy overview explains it in plain language, and the privacy policy has every detail.
href: /privacy-overview.html
