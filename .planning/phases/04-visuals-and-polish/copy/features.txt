title: Features | atHand
description: What atHand does on iPhone: records for your household, document scanning, suggestions, app lock, and encrypted backups.
# FE3
Features
# FE4
atHand stores the details you look up all the time and brings up the right record when it can help.
# C1
Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.
# FE5
Records for everything you look after
# FE6
Add records for people, vehicles, pets, homes, financial accounts, and contacts, or make a custom record with your own labels.
# FE7
Each detail is stored once, on the record it belongs to. A person holds identity, medical, insurance, and document details. A vehicle holds its plate, VIN, insurance, and roadside assistance number. A pet holds its microchip number, vet, and vaccine records.
# FE8
Contacts, such as a doctor, a mechanic, or a vet, are records of their own, and you can link one to any person, pet, or vehicle.
# FE9
Adding a record takes you through one section at a time, and you can save and close at any step. To make a change later, pick the section you want to edit.
# FE10
Scan instead of typing
# FE11
Scan a driver’s license, passport, insurance card, VIN, vaccine record, utility bill, payment card, or business card.
# FE12
atHand reads the barcode on a driver’s license and the machine-readable lines in a passport, and suggests matches from the text on other documents. Recognition runs on your iPhone.
# FE13
Nothing is saved until you check it. atHand marks any field it is unsure of with “Check this,” and you can correct any field or retake the photo before saving.
# FE14
Start with Scan to add on the home screen and atHand works out what kind of record the document belongs to. It recognizes licenses, passports, VINs, and contact QR codes, and asks you to choose for anything else.
# FE15
The right record at the right time
# FE16
atHand can suggest a record when it is likely to help, such as before an appointment on your calendar.
# FE17
Suggestions use the time of day, whether it is a weekday or the weekend, the records you opened recently, and, if you allow them, your calendar and location. Matching runs on your iPhone.
# FE18
If you allow location, Apple’s geocoding service receives your location to return the name of a nearby place. Read how atHand handles your information.
href: /privacy-overview.html
# FE19
The suggestion banner says only that your information is ready. It never names a category, such as medical. When nothing matches with confidence, atHand shows no banner, and you can turn suggestions off at any time.
# FE20
Locked to you
# FE21
Lock atHand with Face ID or Touch ID, with your device passcode as the fallback. There is no separate password to remember.
# FE22
The lock is on by default when your iPhone has Face ID, Touch ID, or a passcode set up. Choose whether atHand locks again as soon as you leave it, or after 5 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1 hour.
# FE23
When you switch away from atHand, a cover hides your information in the app switcher, whether or not the lock is on.
# FE24
Sensitive numbers, such as passport numbers, account numbers, alarm codes, and Wi-Fi passwords, stay hidden until you tap Reveal, and hide again when you leave the app.
# FE25
Backups you control
# FE26
Export an encrypted backup of your records and photos, protected by a passphrase you choose, and save it wherever you like.
# FE27
A backup is a one-time copy, not sync. Importing one replaces all the data on that iPhone, and atHand asks you to confirm first.
# FE28
A backup never includes your purchase, your settings, or your app lock setting.
# FE29
Light and dark
# FE30
atHand follows your iPhone’s appearance, or you can choose Light or Dark in Settings.
