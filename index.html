atHand

Your info, ready when you need it

atHand keeps the details you look up all the time, such as people, vehicles, pets, homes, financial accounts and contacts, in one place on your iPhone.

It is coming to the App Store soon.

How it works

You add your reference information to atHand, or scan documents like a driver’s license, passport, insurance card, VIN or vaccine record with your camera.

Everything stays on your iPhone. Scanning runs on the device, so your images are not sent anywhere.

atHand uses signals on your device to bring up the records that are likely to matter right now. Calendar and location signals are optional.

You can lock the app with Face ID or your device passcode, and export an encrypted backup protected by a passphrase only you know.

Privacy in short

  • Your data is stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • The developer runs no server and cannot see your information.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
