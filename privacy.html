Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

atHand is built so that your personal information stays on your iPhone. The developer collects nothing, operates no server, and has no ability to access your data.

Who this applies to

atHand is published by Jacob Davis, an individual developer in New York, United States. In this policy, “I” and “the developer” mean Jacob Davis.

What the developer collects

Nothing. I do not collect, receive, or have access to any of your personal information.

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • I operate no server, so there is nowhere for your data to be sent.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting tools.

What is stored on your device

The information you add to atHand, such as people, vehicles, pets, homes, financial accounts and contacts, is stored only on your iPhone. It lives in an app-private database and app-private file storage, which other apps cannot read. Nothing is uploaded.

Document scanning

You can scan documents such as driver’s licenses, passports, insurance cards, VINs and vaccine records. Scanning runs entirely on your device using Google ML Kit’s on-device text and barcode recognition. Images are not sent anywhere.

Optional permissions

atHand works without these permissions. You can turn each one on or off at any time in iPhone Settings.

Calendar

If you allow it, atHand compares calendar event titles with a local keyword table on your device, to help surface relevant records. Event data is not stored or transmitted.

Location

If you allow it, your device works out a place category on the device. The coordinates are discarded once the category is determined. Location is never stored or transmitted.

Face ID or device passcode

You can lock the app with Face ID or your device passcode. Your biometric data stays in Apple’s secure hardware and is never seen by the app.

Backups

You can export an encrypted backup file protected by a passphrase you choose, and share it wherever you like. I have no access to the file or to your passphrase, and I cannot recover either one. If you lose the passphrase, the backup cannot be opened.

Third parties

These are the outside services involved, and what each does.

  • Apple processes the one-time in-app purchase and provides Face ID, the passcode lock, and the App Store. Apple’s handling of your data is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat verifies that your purchase unlocks the app. RevenueCat’s handling of purchase data is covered by RevenueCat’s privacy policy.
  • Google ML Kit is used only for on-device text and barcode recognition when you scan. It runs on your device, and your images are not sent to Google through the app.

Apple and RevenueCat receive purchase data under their own policies. No personal information from inside the app is sent to either of them.

Retention and deletion

Because your data exists only on your device, you control how long it is kept. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device. Backup files you exported are separate. Delete those yourself from wherever you saved them.

Children

atHand is not directed at children under 13. The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update it on this page and change the date at the top. Any change that affects how your data is handled will be described here.
