Support

Need help with atHand? Email support@athandapp.com.

What to include in your message

  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The atHand version, if you know it.
  • Any error message you saw, word for word.

Please do not send passphrases, document images, or other sensitive details. I do not need them to help.

Common questions

How do backups work?

You can export an encrypted backup file, protected by a passphrase you choose. You then share the file wherever you want to keep it. The developer never receives the file.

What happens if I lose my backup passphrase?

The backup cannot be opened. The developer has no access to your passphrase and cannot recover it or the backup. Store your passphrase somewhere safe.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open atHand on a device signed in to the Apple ID you used to buy it, and choose the restore purchases option. Apple processes the purchase, and RevenueCat verifies it. If it does not work, email me.

How do I turn on the app lock?

Turn on the app lock in atHand and choose Face ID or your device passcode. Your device must have one of them set up in iPhone Settings.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. This removes all of its data from the device. Backup files you exported are stored separately, so delete those as well if you no longer want them.

Which permissions are optional?

Calendar and location access are both optional. atHand works without them, and you can change them at any time in iPhone Settings. Camera use for scanning and Face ID for the lock are also your choice. See the privacy policy for what each one does.
